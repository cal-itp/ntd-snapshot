# Agency Report Summary
The Agency Report provides an agency-level overview of transit spending, service delivery, funding composition, financial performance, and cost-effectiveness using National Transit Database (NTD) data. The analysis examines transit performance at both the agency and mode levels, where appropriate, while providing agency-level detail to explore differences in agency characteristics, expenditure patterns, and service performance.

Each report focuses on a selected Regional Transportation Planning Agency (RTPA), providing an overall view of transit performance across its agencies. Interactive selections allow users to explore individual agencies within the RTPA, including agency profiles, historical trends, expenditure and funding composition, spending efficiency, fare revenue, and service performance. Where applicable, comparisons also incorporate rural and urban classifications, while operating structures summarize service delivery across directly operated and purchased transportation categories. 

*Analytical Sample Disclaimer: The agency-level analysis includes available agency observations in the NTD data for each reporting year. Results are not separately segmented by reporter type (Building, Full, Reduced Asset, Reduced, and Rural Reporter), reporting module, or agency status unless explicitly specified in the chart. The composition of available agency observations by these characteristics is presented in the **Analytical Sample Composition chart** at the beginning of **mode report** and should be considered when interpreting differences in spending, service delivery, performance, and cost-effectiveness. Operating structure is summarized into directly operated and purchased transportation, with purchased transportation combining General, Taxi, and Transportation Network Company services.*

## Agency Profile
This interactive profile provides an overview of the selected Regional Transportation Planning Agency (RTPA) and the transit agencies within its jurisdiction. It summarizes agency characteristics, NTD reporting information, service area details, and operating structures.

*How to read the chart*
- **RTPA Profile:** Shows the selected `RTPA name`, `counties`, `Caltrans districts`, `total number of agencies`, and `reporting year range`.
- **Agency Selection:** Use the dropdown menu to select an individual agency within the RTPA.
- **Agency:** Shows the `agency name` and `NTD ID`, along with its city and state.
- **Agency Status:** Indicates whether the agency is currently active or inactive in the latest reporting year and summarizes its historical status over the available reporting period.
- **Reporter Type:** Shows the agency's NTD reporter classification in the latest reporting year and how its classification has changed over time, if applicable.
- **Urbanized Area:** Shows the primary `Urbanized Area (UZA)` associated with the agency and its `population` in the latest reporting year.
- **Agency Operating Structure**: Shows the percentage of `total Vehicle Revenue Hours (VRH)` provided through directly operated and purchased transportation services in the latest reporting year. Directly operated services are provided by the agency itself, while purchased transportation represents services provided through contracts with external providers.

### 1.1 Agency Trends in Spending, Funding, and Service
This interactive dashboard presents historical trends in `transit service`, `operating and capital expenditures`, and `funding` for agencies within the selected RTPA. It allows users to examine changes over time at both the regional and individual agency levels.

*How to read the chart*
- **X-axis:** Reporting years.
- **Y-axis:** Represents the value of the selected measure, including `UPT`, `OPEX`, `CAPEX`, `Operating Funding`, `Capital Funding`, `VRH` or `VRM`.
- **Trend:** Changes over time show how transit ridership, expenditures, funding, and service delivery evolve across reporting years.
- **Agency Selection:** Use the dropdown to select an individual agency or view all agencies within the selected RTPA.

## 1.2 Expenditure Composition by Mode for Selected Agency and Year
This dashboard compares the composition of operating and capital expenditures across transit mode groups for a selected agency and reporting year within the RTPA. Operating and capital expenditure charts have separate agency and year selections. Percentages are normalized within each mode group, allowing comparison of expenditure composition rather than absolute spending levels.

*How to read the chart*
- **X-axis:** Transit mode groups.
- **Y-axis:** Represents each mode group's share of total operating or capital expenditures, expressed as a percentage.
- **Stacked Bars:** Each segment represents an expenditure component, showing its proportion of the total expenditure within that mode group.
- **Operating Expenditures:** Includes `vehicle operations`, `vehicle maintenance`, `non-vehicle maintenance`, and `general administration`.
- **Capital Expenditures:** Includes `rolling stock`, `facilities`, and `other capital expenditures`.
- **Agency Selection:** Use the agency dropdown to select an individual agency within the RTPA.
- **Year Selection:** Select a reporting year to examine expenditure composition for that year.

## 1.3 Funding Composition for Selected Agency and Year
This interactive chart examines the composition of operating and capital funding for agencies within the selected RTPA, allowing users to compare the contributions of federal, state, local, and other funding sources. Funding percentages are normalized separately within operating and capital funding, so each bar represents the relative contribution of funding sources to its respective funding total rather than absolute funding amounts.

*How to read the chart*
- **X-axis:** Funding types — `Operating Funding` and `Capital Funding`.
- **Y-axis:** Represents each funding type's share of total funding, expressed as a percentage.
- **Stacked Bars:** Each segment represents the contribution of a funding source (Federal, State, Local, or Other) to the respective funding type.
- **Agency Selection:** Use the dropdown to select an individual agency within the RTPA.
- **Year Selection:** Select a reporting year to examine funding composition for that year.

## 1.4 Operating Cost Efficiency by Mode for Selected Year and Reporting Module 
This interactive dashboard compares agency-level operating cost efficiency across Urban and Rural peer groups within the selected RTPA. Users can select a reporting year and transit mode to examine agency rankings across three measures: `OPEX per VRH`, `OPEX per VRM`, and `OPEX per UPT`. Agencies are ranked separately within Urban and Rural peer groups. Lower cost values indicate lower operating expenses per unit of service or ridership, rather than necessarily indicating better overall agency performance.

*How to read the chart*
- **X-axis:** Operating cost per unit of service or ridership, expressed in dollars.
- **Y-axis:** Transit agencies, ranked by the selected efficiency measure.
- **Urban and Rural Panels:** Agencies are grouped by their rural or urban classification to support comparisons within similar peer groups.
- **Year Selection:** Select a reporting year to compare agency rankings over time.
- **Mode Selection:** Select a transit mode to examine operating cost efficiency for that mode.

## 1.5 OPEX Mode Performance (HHI, Spending Per Capita and Volatility)
This interactive dashboard examines agency operating expense performance within the selected RTPA, comparing agencies across Rural and Urban reporting modules. Users can select an `agency`, `reporting year``, and `transit mode` to explore three performance measures: `Expense Concentration (HHI)`, `Spending per Capita`, and `Spending Volatility`. The dashboard combines agency-specific indicators with peer comparisons to provide context for evaluating differences in operating expense patterns.

*How to read the chart*
- **Measures:** Each scorecard shows `Expense Concentration (HHI)`, `Spending per Capita`, and `Spending Volatility`.
- **Expense Concentration:** Measured using the `Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI)`. Higher values indicate that spending is concentrated in fewer expenditure components, while lower values indicate a more evenly distributed spending composition.
- **Spending per Capita:** Shows average agency-level operating expenditure per capita across agencies within each reporting module.
- **Spending Volatility:** Shows the average absolute change in spending between the selected reporting year and the preceding year. Higher values indicate greater year-over-year fluctuations in spending.
- **Peer Comparison:** Compares the selected agency against other agencies within the same Rural or Urban reporting module.
- **Selected Agency:** Highlighted with a `diamond marker`, while the `reference marker` indicates the peer group median.
- **Agency, Year, and Mode Selection:** Use the dropdowns to select the agency, reporting year, and transit mode for analysis.

## 1.7 Agency Operating and Service Performance Growth

This interactive dashboard examines year-over-year growth in operating expenditures, capital expenditures, fleet size, service delivery, ridership, and fare revenue across agencies within the selected RTPA. Users can select an agency, transit mode, and reporting year to explore changes in seven measures: `Real OPEX`, `Real CAPEX`, `Vehicles Operated in Maximum Service (VOMS)`, `Vehicle Revenue Hours`, `Vehicle Revenue Miles`, `UPT`, and `Real Fare Revenue`. Growth rates represent the percentage change between the selected reporting year and the preceding year. Expenditure and fare revenue measures are adjusted for inflation to reflect real changes over time.

*How to read the chart*
- **X-axis:** Performance measures.
- **Y-axis:** Year-over-year percentage change.
- **Bars:** Show the percentage increase or decrease in each measure.
- **Positive Values:** Indicate an increase relative to the previous year.
- **Negative Values:** Indicate a decrease relative to the previous year.
- **Agency Selection:** Select an individual agency within the RTPA.
- **Mode Selection:** Select a transit mode to examine performance changes for that mode.
- **Year Selection:** Select a reporting year to examine changes relative to the preceding year.

Note: Changes in these measures reflect year-over-year differences and do not necessarily indicate improvements or declines in overall agency performance.

## 1.8 Farebox Recovery and Subsidy Relationship
This interactive dashboard examines the relationship between `farebox recovery` and `operating subsidy` across transit agencies and mode groups within the selected RTPA. It allows users to explore differences in fare revenue coverage and reliance on operating subsidies.

*How to read the chart*
- **X-axis:** Farebox recovery ratio, representing the share of operating expenses covered by fare revenue.
- **Y-axis:** Operating subsidy per unit of service, as defined in the chart.
- **Points:** Represent individual agency-mode observations.
- **Comparison:** The distribution of points highlights differences in fare revenue recovery and subsidy reliance across agencies and mode groups.

Note: Higher farebox recovery indicates greater coverage of operating expenses through fare revenue, while higher subsidy values indicate greater reliance on operating subsidies.

## 1.9 Agency Size and Funding Relationship: RTPA vs. Statewide
This interactive chart examines the relationship between agency size, measured by operating and capital expenditures, and funding composition across Federal, State, Local, and Other sources. It compares agencies within the selected RTPA against statewide agency observations to explore whether funding patterns vary with agency size.

*How to read the chart*
- **X-axis:** Agency size, measured using the relevant expenditure values.
- **Y-axis:** Funding share (%) from Federal, State, Local, or Other sources.
- **RTPA:** Shows the relationship between agency expenditure levels and funding shares among agencies within the selected RTPA.
- **Statewide:** Provides a statewide comparison of the relationship. (Gray points)
- **Year Selection:** Allows users to examine relationships for different reporting years.
- **Trendline:** Shows the direction of the relationship between agency size and funding share.

**Interpretation Note:** The trendline slope indicates the direction of the relationship, while R² indicates how much variation in funding share is explained by agency size. For example, a statewide slope of +0.06 and R² of 0.05 indicate a slight positive relationship, but agency size explains only 5% of the variation in funding share. This suggests little linear association between the variables.

**Analytical Sample Disclaimer:** The analysis uses available agency-level observations, and sample sizes may be limited for certain RTPAs and reporting years. Small sample sizes can make trendlines less reliable. Results, particularly those with low R² values, should be interpreted with caution and should not be taken as evidence of a meaningful relationship between agency size and funding composition.
